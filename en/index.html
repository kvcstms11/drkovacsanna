I am Dr. Anna Kovács — a general practitioner specialised in minimally invasive aesthetic medicine. I completed my medical studies at the University of Szeged and the Medical University of Vienna, then worked as a GP in Lower Austria and Styria, with emergency medicine certification.

My goal is to gently reduce the traces of time — making the appearance fresher, more rested and harmonious, without losing natural beauty. As one of three recipients worldwide of the AAAM 25th Anniversary Scholarship, I bring internationally recognised expertise.
