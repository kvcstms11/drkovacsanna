My name is Dr. Anna Kovács, and I am a physician with a great passion for aesthetic treatments. For me, aesthetics means far more than a beautiful appearance — it is about preserving and subtly underlining a person's natural radiance.

In my work I combine medical expertise with an individual, empathetic approach. Through numerous training courses in Austria and abroad I have been able to deepen and broaden my knowledge continuously.

It matters to me to see every patient as an individual, to advise carefully and to shape treatments so that they look harmonious, elegant and authentic. My aim is natural results that do not change you, but gently highlight your own beauty and strengthen your wellbeing in the long term.

Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions
at a glance.

What is the difference between Botox and fillers?

Botulinum toxin relaxes the muscles that cause expression lines. Hyaluronic acid fillers replace lost volume and fill static folds. The two often complement each other – we will clarify what makes sense for you during the consultation. More about Botox →

How long do the results last?

It depends on the method: botulinum toxin lasts around three to four months, hyaluronic acid fillers nine to eighteen months depending on the area, and skin boosters roughly six to nine months. Biostimulators build up more slowly but often last beyond a year. See the price list →

Are the treatments painful?

Most patients describe the treatments as easily tolerable. Where appropriate I use numbing cream, and many fillers also contain a local anaesthetic. Read more →

What side effects can occur?

Slight redness, swelling or small bruises at the injection sites are possible and usually settle within a few days. I explain rarer risks personally and in plain language before every treatment. More on safety →

How do I know whether a treatment is right for me?

We decide that together during the medical consultation, after analysing your face and skin. If I cannot recommend a treatment, I will say so openly. How the consultation works →

What happens at the first consultation?

We talk through your wishes, your medical history and what makes sense medically. You receive an honest assessment, a treatment proposal and a binding price – with no pressure to decide on the spot. Read more →
